Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how tavascript Publisher (the "App"), operated by the tavascript creator ("we"), handles information. The App is a private publishing tool used only to upload tavascript's own original videos to tavascript's own accounts.

Information we access

  • TikTok: when the account owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, we receive an access token and basic account information (such as open ID, display name and avatar) for the authorized tavascript account, and we use the Content Posting API to upload and publish videos to that account.
  • Instagram and YouTube: the App uses the official APIs to publish videos to tavascript's own accounts and to read aggregate performance statistics of those videos.

The App does not access, collect or process data of any other TikTok, Instagram or YouTube users, and it does not read private messages, contacts or followers' personal data.

How we use it

Only to publish tavascript's own videos (with captions and privacy settings chosen by the creator) and to review how those videos perform.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer in a local file that is not shared or published. We do not run a public server that stores user data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Information is sent only to the platform it belongs to (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) through their official APIs.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as needed to publish videos. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), and we delete stored tokens on request.

Children

The App is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the tavascript creator.
